1. Overview

Krystalgraphix built ScratchTopUp as a free application. This Service is provided by Krystalgraphix at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs users regarding policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information for ScratchTopUp users.

2. Information Collection and Device Permissions

ScratchTopUp requires access to specific device features to perform airtime scratch card scanning and automated dialer operations:

  • Camera Access: Used solely to scan voucher/scratch card numbers in real-time. No camera frames or images are saved or transmitted off your device.
  • Phone/Call State Permission: Used to initiate USSD code requests directly for mobile top-ups.
